About Us

Connecting students with the people, ideas, and career paths shaping the sports industry.

Our mission

CMC Sports Business Club creates opportunities for students to explore the business side of sports through professional development, speaker events, networking, and hands-on learning.

Access

We bring students closer to alumni, professionals, and organizations across the sports industry.

Education

We help members learn how careers in sports business actually work, from entry-level roles to long-term paths.

Community

We create a space for students who love sports and business to connect across interests and class years.
